# Changelog

All notable changes to the usecalendar packages are documented here. The packages follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/) and are versioned independently. Per-package changelogs will be generated by changesets once release automation lands ([#13](https://github.com/SebastianUrdaneguiBisalaya/usecalendar/issues/13)).

## Unreleased

First release, `0.1.0` of both packages.

### `@usecalendar/core`

- `CalendarEvent<TData>`, `DateRange` and `TimeRange` types, with instants compared by time, never as text.
- Views and navigation: `visibleDays`, `visibleRange`, `shiftDate`, `startOfWeek`, week starts (`monday`, `sunday`, `saturday`).
- Month layout (weeks, ISO week numbers, multi-day lanes) and day layout (overlapping timed events).
- Drag and resize math: `moveRange`, `resizeRange`, `sweepRange`, snapping.
- Keyboard focus models for the time grid, the month grid and the date strip (`nextFocus`, `nextMonthFocus`, `nextStripFocus`).
- Date strip model for the mini calendar: `stripStart`, `stripDays`, `shiftStrip`, `addStripDays`, `isDayInRange`, `clampDay`.
- Shortcut keymap (`CALENDAR_KEYMAP`) with `resolveKeymap`, `matchesHotkey`, `describeHotkey` and `ariaKeyShortcut`.

### `@usecalendar/react`

- `<Calendar>`: toolbar plus month, week and day views, controlled or uncontrolled, with `onRangeChange` for fetching.
- `MonthView`, `TimeGridView`, `CalendarToolbar`, `EventChip` and `EventBlock` for custom layouts, with `useCalendar` and `CalendarProvider`.
- Creating, moving and resizing events by pointer and keyboard, reported through `onSlotClick`, `onRangeSelect` and `onEventChange`.
- Render props: `renderEvent`, `renderAllDayEvent`, `renderMonthEvent`, `renderEventWrapper`, `renderMorePopover`.
- `<MiniCalendar>` and `useMiniCalendar` under the `@usecalendar/react/mini` entry: a horizontal day strip with paging, `days="auto"`, limits, sync with a full calendar through `calendar`, and `renderDay`.
- Remappable keyboard shortcuts (`hotkeys`, `hotkeysScope`, `hotkeyHandlers`) and WAI-ARIA grid and listbox semantics.
- Localization through `locale`, `timeFormat`, `labels` and `formatters`.
- Theming through `--uc-*` tokens that fall back to shadcn/ui tokens (dark mode included), `className` and `classNames`. `theme.css` is for Tailwind v4 apps and `styles.css` for apps without Tailwind.
